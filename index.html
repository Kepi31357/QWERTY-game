Features · How to Play

Simple to start. Sharp to master.

Four moves to know — the rest is wordcraft and a race to 1000.

  1. Start on green Your first word covers the green YOU square. Opponents start on amber P2.
  2. Connect every play New words share a letter with the board — classic crossword rules.
  3. Chase gold stars Land on a star for +50. First to 1000 points wins the match.
  4. Play your way Solo vs Computer (Easy / Medium / Hard) or invite a friend with a room code and chat.
